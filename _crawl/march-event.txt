#1 March 15 Chicago Food Packing Event
                - 
#4 Anuj Saxena
                    15 Mar 2026
                        &bull; 3 min read
        Share
        We (and you) made a difference!
#2 500 sandwiches + 150 brown bags - Night Ministry
(help people who are facing homelessness, poverty, or crisis in Chicagoland area)
#2 200 bags with 6 different items - Palatine High School
(for ~50 students over a 4 weekend period)
Our Youth Leaders for this event were Amaya Arora and Aarush Mallu.
Here are some pictures from the event . Join us next time on April 15, 2026 and make a difference!
Thanks to everyone that sent money via Zelle and our online donation link. We bought items on your behalf
All the items are ready for donations!
Items were donated to Palatine High School. While delivering the packages, Palatine HS coordinator shared that our bags came in just in time as their shelf was completely empty. In addition, she will give each kid 2 bags so that they have some meals during spring break next week when they are not in school.
Special thanks to our junior board members - Aurav Shanmugam, Vikram Pai, and Mihir Binaykia - who came after school to load, unload and transport the 200 bags we packed to Palatine High School.
Night Ministry came and picked up the food earlier this week and they were really grateful for the 500+ sandwiches and 150 brown bags we packed.
