September 17, 2026 - Thank you Palatine High School for featuring our work on your IG page delivering packed food from our Sept. 13 food packing event. 
SBF team dropping food items at Palatine High School
September 14, 2026 - SBF India provided 13,000+ meals to villages/slums in suburbs of New Delhi. Eight items will be packed in 101 meal kits for families. This will feed a family of 4 for ~15 days 
August 30, 2026 - SBF Chicago hit a milestone. We have packed 10,200+ meals so far in Chicagoland and India
August 15, 2026 - We have been added to Ela Area Public Library (Lake Zurich) teen volunteering site! 
June 12, 2026 - Partnered with Emmaus House of Hospitality in Lake Zurich to receive excess (food) items for donation
June 8, 2026 - SBF Junior Board gave a presentation about SBF’s mission and how to team with the club for future opportunities
June 3, 2026 - SBF participated in volunteer fair at Ela Public Library in Lake Zurich where we networked and talked about SBF and explore partnership opportunities. 
June 1, 2026 - SBF Junior Board participated at the Youth Fair organized by Rep. Syed at Ela Public Library
May 4, 2026 - SBF Junior Board had the opportunity to meet with IL State Representative Nabeela Syed during her monthly office hours at the Palatine Public Library and share the work that SBF Chicago has been doing to address food insecurity locally in Chicagoland area as well as globally. 
#2 A word from our partners...
This is what our partners have told us about the impact SBF Chicago has made to the community
"Because of your generosity, 150 bags were able to reach families and individuals who truly needed them. Half were distributed at the Lexington Hotel, where many individuals experiencing homelessness are currently staying, and the other half were shared along the river and throughout the park with many of our homeless friends in the community reside in." - Chancez NFP (May 21, 2026)
"Thank you Rani and Rashmi for partnering with FOOD FOR FRIENDS and providing 150 sack lunches of PBJ, power bar and chips for the homeless men at the Annex Hotel on Clarke Street in the downtown loop of Chicago" - Dan, Founder of Foods For Friends (May 21, 2006)
