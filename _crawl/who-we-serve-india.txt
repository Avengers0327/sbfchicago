Project 1: Blind School (Lunch) 
Feeding of ~50-75 girls who are partially blind/completely blind in blind school located in New Delhi
Project 2: Gau Bhoj (feeding cows) 
Feeding of 6 cows in Gahiri village, Pratapgarh, U.P. to help sustain local villages with milk, milk products, organic manure, biogas, etc.
Project 3: Labor Chowk (lunch) 
Lunch for ~150/200 daily laborers + kids + families at Labor Chowk in Gurugram (New Delhi)
Project 4: Two Slum Schools (lunch) 
Feeding lunch for ~100 children in 2 slums in New Delhi
Project 5: Apna Ghar (lunch) 
Feeding of ~500 people (5 units of homeless/orphans, each unit of ~100 people) in New Delhi
Project 6: Animal Rescue Shelter 
Feeding dogs, birds, cows, rabbits at animal rescue shelter in Gurugram
Project 7: Lunch for Orphanage in Shirdi
Feeding 5 kg of Masala Rice and 5 kg of Halwa (Sheera) to an orphanage (~150 kids and staff) in Shirdi
