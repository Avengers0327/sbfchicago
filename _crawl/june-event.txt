        We packed 1250+ sandwiches and 650 brown bags at our June food packing event! Thanks for joining hand to help feed the needy.
Special shoutout to National India Hub for hosting us and their partnership.
#2 500 sandwiches + 500 brown bags
                    (help people who are facing homelessness, poverty, or crisis in Chicagoland area)
#2 625+ sandwiches + 150 brown bags
                    (help people who are facing homelessness, poverty, or crisis in Chicagoland area)
Our Youth Leader for this event was Shaurya Middela. This was our first event at National India Hub and we had a great turnout. We had over 45+ people helping to pack 1250+ meals! We could not have done this without your support. 
Through our partnership with Food For Friends, we were able to distribute the meals we packed to Chancez NFP and House of Restoration Community Outreach. They are both doing great work helping the underserved in Elgin, IL and surrounding area. We also provided meals to the Annex Men's Hotel - a men's homeless shelter in the city of Chicago. More information for all these organizations can be found here. 
https://www.foodforfriends.org/
https://chanceznfp.org/
https://www.houseofrestorationinc.org/
