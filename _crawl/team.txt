SB Foundation (SBF Chicago): Youth-powered. Community-fueled. Hunger-fought.
We're a youth-led nonprofit in Chicago's Northwest suburbs on a mission to fight hunger — from communities in Chicagoland and India. We turn volunteering into action: packing sandwiches, snacks, and meal bags that reach local schools and charities serving families facing food insecurity. Simple to join, easy to feel, hard to forget.
Students. Families. Groups. Anyone ready to show up. Pack a meal bag, support a donation drive in India, or just bring your hands and your heart — every event is a chance to make a real difference, together.
PACK. FEED. MAKE A DIFFERENCE
#2 Ashna Saxena, Co-President/Co-Founder
                    Ashna Saxena is a junior at William Fremd High School and the Co-President/Co-Founder of SBF Chicago. Because of her strong passion for community service and leadership, she has been actively volunteering from a young age alongside her family, supporting multiple organizations focused on addressing food insecurity. Her involvement include cooking meals for the women’s shelter at Breakthrough Ministry in Garfield Park, helping prepare and package meals at her local temple for Port Ministries, and organizing food drives for the Palatine Township Food Pantry. These experiences inspired her to take a more active role in addressing hunger on a larger scale. Through SBF, she works to expand initiatives that fight food insecurity both locally and globally. #2 Aurav Shanmugam, Co-President/Co-Founder
                    Aurav Shanmugam is a junior at William Fremd High School and the Co-President/Co-Founder of SBF Chicago. Aurav’s commitment to community service began early through his involvement with Clean Up Give Back in Palatine, where he developed a passion for environmental work and giving back. He has also volunteered at local temples, taking on leadership roles in organizing food drives. After becoming aware of students in his school district who lack access to food on weekends, Aurav recognized a pressing need in his community. Motivated to make a difference, he worked to co-found SBF Chicago to help address food insecurity and create meaningful local impact. He has hands-on experience running his own landscaping business, where he manages operations, leads teams, and makes strategic decisions.
