We provide many opportunities for our young leaders to come and help with this initiative.
- Volunteer Hours will be provided to students for Chicago food packing events as follows: - Leads: 4 hours (includes developing presentations, marketing event, recruiting for event, buying food items, helping set-up/clean-up, supervising on day of event)
- Regular: 2 hours (includes helping set-up/clean-up, packing items, buying food items)
- Other opportunities to help include:- Dropping off items to shelters
- Marketing SBF activities
- Website updates
- Quarterly newsletter
- ...and more!
