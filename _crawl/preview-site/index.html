Who we are

SBF Vision and Team

SB Foundation (SBF Chicago): Youth-powered. Community-fueled. Hunger-fought.

We're a youth-led nonprofit in Chicago's Northwest suburbs on a mission to fight hunger — from communities in Chicagoland and India. We turn volunteering into action: packing sandwiches, snacks, and meal bags that reach local schools and charities serving families facing food insecurity. Simple to join, easy to feel, hard to forget.

Students. Families. Groups. Anyone ready to show up. Pack a meal bag, support a donation drive in India, or just bring your hands and your heart — every event is a chance to make a real difference, together.

Ashna Saxena, Co-President/Co-Founder

Ashna Saxena is a junior at William Fremd High School and the Co-President/Co-Founder of SBF Chicago. Because of her strong passion for community service and leadership, she has been actively volunteering from a young age alongside her family, supporting multiple organizations focused on addressing food insecurity. Her involvement include cooking meals for the women’s shelter at Breakthrough Ministry in Garfield Park, helping prepare and package meals at her local temple for Port Ministries, and organizing food drives for the Palatine Township Food Pantry. These experiences inspired her to take a more active role in addressing hunger on a larger scale. Through SBF, she works to expand initiatives that fight food insecurity both locally and globally. 2 volunteer hours4 hours for youth leadsStudents, families and groups

    Make sandwiches

    Peanut butter and jelly, assembly-line style, hundreds at a time.

    Pack the bags

    Brown bags with a sandwich, chips and a granola bar, plus weekend meal bags for students.

  4. Step 4

    Deliver

    Partners pick up, and our team drops bags at shelters and high schools.

August 9 Chicago Food Packing Event

By Anuj Saxena3 min read

We packed 1200+ sandwiches and 650 brown bags at our August food packing event! Thanks to all those that could join us and/or supported us by donating money/sending items from our Amazon Wishlist.

Special shoutout to National India Hub for hosting us and their partnership.

500 sandwiches + 500 brown bags

(help people who are facing homelessness, poverty, or crisis in Chicagoland area)

700+ sandwiches + 150 brown bags

(help people who are facing homelessness, poverty, or crisis in Chicagoland area)

The Youth Leaders for this event were our Junior Board and Executive Team members. We had over 60+ people helping to pack 1200+ meals! We could not have done this without your support.

Through our partnership with Food For Friends, we were able to distribute the meals we packed to Chancez NFP and House of Restoration Community Outreach. They are both doing great work helping the underserved in Elgin, IL and surrounding area. We also provided meals to the Annex Men's Hotel - a men's homeless shelter in the city of Chicago.
